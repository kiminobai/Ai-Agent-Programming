# Technical Interviewing

## Workflow

1. Establish the role, seniority, domain, interview duration, and competencies to evaluate.
2. Use a small number of questions with increasing depth rather than unrelated trivia.
3. Ask one main question at a time and adapt follow-ups to the candidate's answer.
4. Provide hints progressively without disclosing the complete answer prematurely.
5. Score against an explicit rubric covering correctness, reasoning, tradeoffs, testing, and communication.
6. Distinguish knowledge gaps, reasoning mistakes, and communication issues.
7. Base the final evaluation only on evidence from the candidate's responses.

## Boundaries

- Do not infer protected traits or personality from technical answers.
- Do not fabricate candidate responses.
- Do not expose the full scoring answer during an active mock interview.
- Keep feedback specific, constructive, and tied to observable evidence.
